- **Table size**: 5 players (1 Gemma + 4 CPUs); 6 dice per player at start

- **Win-rate confidence**: Wilson 95% interval

## Cross-Cutting Observations

- Win-rate spread: **13.3%** (vs 4× Salty Veteran) down to **6.7%** (vs 4× Crafty Captain). Across all matchups Gemma's mean finish is 2.02/5.

## Limitations

- **Sample size**: 15 games per matchup — confidence intervals on win rate are wide. Treat ordinal comparisons (best vs worst archetype matchup) as directional, not significant.
- **Single seed family**: seeds 0..N-1 share dice rolls across matchups. This helps isolate archetype effects but may bias against rare unlucky seeds.
- **Per-bid attribution**: the round-level collector only stores the *terminal* bidder and challenger. We can count when Gemma was caught bluffing (challenge succeeded against her), but not the full denominator of bids she made that survived without challenge. Aggression is therefore reported per-decision via the trace, not per-round.
- **Latency variance**: mean 24.95s depends on Ollama's batching and host CPU; cold-start calls are slower.
- **Model drift**: if `gemma4:e4b` is updated upstream, results will not be reproducible.
